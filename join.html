Membership Categories

🌟 Founding Members

Individuals who engineered and birthed the existence of the Association

👥 Ordinary Members

Individuals who register and actively participate in activities

🎓 Student Members

Young people in tertiary institutions joining through campus chapters

🤝 Associate Members

Supporters who contribute financially or morally

✓ Rights of Members

  • Vote and be voted for (excluding honorary/associate)
  • Participate in meetings and decision-making
  • Access training, mentorship, and empowerment programs

📋 Duties of Members

  • Uphold the Constitution and Code of Ethics
  • Pay membership dues as determined
  • Participate in mobilization and advocacy
  • Promote unity within the Association

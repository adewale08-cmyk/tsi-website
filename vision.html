WHY CONTINUITY MATTERS

The 2027 Vision: A Stable, Prosperous, and Renewed Nigeria

⚠️ The Risk of Reversal

Key projects and fiscal gains since 2023 risk being halted if direction changes. The progress we've made is real, but it is also fragile without continued leadership.

📚 Protect Student Future

Continue and expand NELFUND support to keep 600,000+ students in school and open access for 2 million more Nigerians.

🏗️ Complete Major Infrastructure

Finish the Lagos-Calabar Coastal Highway and expand the 30+ major projects across all six geopolitical zones.

💰 Deepen Economic Reforms

Expand tax reforms to 15% of GDP and double Foreign Direct Investment to $100B.

🛡️ Sustain Anti-Corruption Momentum

Continue unprecedented EFCC conviction rates and institutional transparency.

🏘️ Empower More LGAs

Deepen the autonomy victory to ensure grassroots development reaches every ward in all 774 LGAs.

💪 Protect Workers' Gains

Build on the 133% minimum wage increase and expand consumer credit access.

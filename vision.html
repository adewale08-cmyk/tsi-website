WHY CONTINUITY MATTERS

The 2027 Vision: A Stable, Prosperous, and Renewed Nigeria

⚠️ The Risk of Reversal

Key projects and fiscal gains since 2023 risk being halted if direction changes. The progress we've made is real, but it is also fragile without continued leadership.

The 2027 Agenda: Building on Success

📚

Expand NELFUND Access

Continue support for 600,000+ current students and expand access to 2 million more Nigerians, ensuring no qualified student is left behind due to cost.

🏗️

Complete Major Infrastructure

Finish the Lagos-Calabar Coastal Highway and expand the 30+ major projects across all six geopolitical zones, creating jobs and connecting markets.

💰

Deepen Economic Reforms

Expand tax reforms to achieve 15% of GDP and double Foreign Direct Investment to $100 billion.

🛡️

Sustain Anti-Corruption Momentum

Continue unprecedented EFCC conviction rates and expand institutional transparency across all levels of government.

🏘️

Deepen LGA Autonomy

Ensure grassroots development reaches every ward in all 774 LGAs through direct funding and accountability mechanisms.

💪

Protect Workers' Gains

Build on the 133% minimum wage increase, expand consumer credit access, and create 5 million new jobs.

🏥

Universal Health Coverage

Expand the National Health Insurance Authority (NHIA) to cover 50 million additional Nigerians.

⚡

Energy Sector Transformation

Achieve 15,000MW generation capacity and expand renewable energy access to rural communities.

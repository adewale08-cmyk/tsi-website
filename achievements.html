Philosophy: Shifting the burden from production to consumption and high earners. Protecting small businesses and low-income earners.

Key Protections

  • SME Exemption: ₦100M turnover threshold - companies below this exempt from corporate income tax
  • Individual Relief: ₦800,000 annual income exemption for low-income earners
  • Landmark reforms: NTA, NTAA, NRSA, and JRBA laws

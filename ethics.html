Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct

"Integrity in Mobilization, Transparency in Service"

Article 1 Article 2 Article 3 Article 4 Article 5 Article 6 Article 7 Article 8 Article 9 Article 10 Declaration
ARTICLE 1

PREAMBLE AND IDENTITY

1.1. The Tinubu Solidarity Initiative (TSI) is a national civic mobilization platform dedicated to advancing the Renewed Hope Agenda through lawful democratic participation, voter education, and grassroots engagement.

1.2. This Code establishes binding standards for all members, officers, volunteers, and representatives across all tiers: National, Zonal, State, LGA, and Ward structures.

1.3. By accepting appointment or membership, all persons agree to uphold this Code, the Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, the Electoral Act 2022 (as amended), and all applicable campaign finance regulations.

ARTICLE 2

CORE VALUES AND FIDUCIARY DUTIES

2.1. Financial Integrity: Fiduciary responsibility over TSI operational resources with zero tolerance for misappropriation, ghost worker creation, or kickback schemes.

2.2. Data Sovereignty: Protection of biometric data, voter information, and personal data of registrants under the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) 2019.

2.3. Electoral Non-Violence: Absolute commitment to peaceful advocacy; prohibition of voter intimidation, ballot interference, or incitement to electoral violence.

2.4. INEC Compliance: Strict adherence to Independent National Electoral Commission regulations.

2.5. Transparency: Openness in expenditure tracking via blockchain systems and biometric verification protocols.

2.6. Inclusivity: Equitable treatment of women (50% representation targets), youth (18-35), persons with disabilities, and all ethnic/religious groups.

ARTICLE 3

FINANCIAL ETHICS AND BUDGET COMPLIANCE

3.1. Budget Fidelity: All expenditures must align with the approved budget allocations.

3.2. Biometric Accountability: All recipients of stipends must be biometrically verified; no cash payments without fingerprint confirmation.

3.3. Blockchain Transparency: All significant expenditures must be recorded on the Hyperledger ledger.

3.4. Prohibition of Personal Enrichment: No officer shall award contracts to companies in which they or family members hold interest.

3.5. Milestone-Based Integrity: No officer shall falsify registration numbers, event attendance figures, or milestone achievements.

3.6. Audit Cooperation: All members must submit to periodic forensic audits.

ARTICLE 4

DATA PROTECTION AND DIGITAL ETHICS

4.1. Biometric Data Protection: Fingerprint templates, facial recognition data, and NIN information shall be encrypted using AES-256 standards.

4.2. Voter Privacy: PVC numbers, phone numbers, and demographic data shall not be used for unsolicited marketing.

4.3. Digital Command Integrity: Social media managers shall label all sponsored content clearly as "TSI Promotional Material".

4.4. Cybersecurity: No member shall share TSI database passwords or blockchain private keys.

ARTICLE 5

ELECTORAL INTEGRITY AND NON-VIOLENCE

5.1. Peaceful Mobilization: TSI prohibits armed bodyguards, thuggery, intimidation, destruction of opposition materials, and incitement of religious or ethnic hatred.

5.2. INEC Cooperation: Members shall assist, not obstruct, INEC officials during voter registration.

5.3. Vote Buying Prohibition: Strict prohibition against distributing money or gifts contingent on voting preference.

5.4. Accurate Representation: No member shall forge INEC registration slips or inflate voter registration numbers.

ARTICLE 6

ORGANIZATIONAL CONDUCT AND HIERARCHY

6.1. Chain of Command: Ward Coordinators report to LGA Coordinators; no unauthorized parallel structures.

6.2. Spokesperson Protocol: Only the National Coordinator and designated Directors may issue official statements.

6.3. Asset Protection: TSI vehicles, motorcycles, and equipment remain Initiative property.

6.4. Intellectual Property: The TSI logo and "Renewed Hope Agenda" branding are proprietary.

ARTICLE 7

CONFLICT OF INTEREST AND DISCLOSURE

7.1. Financial Disclosure: All National Command members and State Coordinators must declare ownership interests in companies bidding for TSI contracts.

7.2. Recusal: Officers must recuse themselves from decisions where personal interests conflict.

7.3. Post-Service Restrictions: Former TSI officers may not lobby for contracts with the Initiative for 12 months after leaving office.

ARTICLE 8

WHISTLEBLOWING AND PROTECTION

8.1. Mandatory Reporting: Members must report suspected ghost workers, financial misappropriation, violence, or data breaches.

8.2. Reporting Channels: Ethics Hotline, Blockchain Tip Line via TSI App, or Direct to Audit Firm.

8.3. Anti-Retaliation: Any member threatening, demoting, or removing a whistleblower faces immediate expulsion.

8.4. Rewards: Members whose reports lead to recovery of stolen funds may receive a percentage of recovered amount.

ARTICLE 9

DISCIPLINARY PROCEDURES AND SANCTIONS

9.1. Ethics Committee: Standing committee comprising Board of Trustees representative, National Command representative, External legal counsel, and 2 Ward Coordinator representatives.

9.4. Appeal Process: Sanctioned members may appeal to the Board of Trustees within 7 days.

9.5. Clawback Provisions: Members found guilty of financial misconduct must repay misappropriated funds with interest.

ARTICLE 10

VOLUNTEER WELFARE AND PROTECTION

10.1. Safety First: TSI shall not deploy volunteers to violent hotspots without security escort.

10.2. Insurance Coverage: All volunteers covered by group personal accident insurance.

10.3. Stipend Protection: Biometrically-verified volunteers receive approved stipends without deductions.

10.4. Non-Discrimination: Equal opportunity for leadership positions; 50% women's representation target.

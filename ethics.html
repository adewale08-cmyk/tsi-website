Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct

"Integrity in Mobilization, Transparency in Service"

Article 1 Article 2 Article 3 Article 4 Article 5 Article 6 Article 7 Article 8 Article 9 Article 10
ARTICLE 1

PREAMBLE AND IDENTITY

1.1. The Tinubu Solidarity Initiative (TSI) is a national civic mobilization platform dedicated to advancing the Renewed Hope Agenda through lawful democratic participation, voter education, and grassroots engagement.

1.2. This Code establishes binding standards for all members, officers, volunteers, and representatives across all tiers.

1.3. By accepting appointment or membership, all persons agree to uphold this Code, the Constitution of Nigeria, and the Electoral Act 2022.

ARTICLE 2

CORE VALUES AND FIDUCIARY DUTIES

2.1. Financial Integrity: Zero tolerance for misappropriation, ghost workers, or kickbacks.

2.2. Data Sovereignty: Protection of biometric and voter data under NDPR 2019.

2.3. Electoral Non-Violence: Prohibition of voter intimidation, ballot interference, or incitement to violence.

2.4. Inclusivity: 50% women's representation target, youth inclusion, disability access.

ARTICLE 3

FINANCIAL ETHICS

3.1. Budget Fidelity: All expenditures must align with approved budget.

3.2. Biometric Accountability: No cash payments without fingerprint confirmation.

3.3. Prohibition of Personal Enrichment: No officer shall award contracts to family-owned businesses.

3.4. Audit Cooperation: All members must submit to periodic forensic audits.

ARTICLE 4

DATA PROTECTION

4.1. Biometric data shall be encrypted using AES-256 standards.

4.2. Voter information shall not be used for unsolicited marketing.

4.3. No member shall share TSI database passwords or blockchain private keys.

ARTICLE 5

ELECTORAL INTEGRITY

5.1. TSI prohibits armed bodyguards, thuggery, and destruction of opposition materials.

5.2. Members shall assist INEC officials, not obstruct them.

5.3. Vote buying is strictly prohibited.

ARTICLE 6

ORGANIZATIONAL CONDUCT

6.1. Ward Coordinators report to LGA Coordinators; no parallel structures.

6.2. Only the National Coordinator may issue official statements.

6.3. TSI assets remain Initiative property.

ARTICLE 7

CONFLICT OF INTEREST

7.1. Members must declare ownership in companies bidding for TSI contracts.

7.2. Recusal required where personal interests conflict.

ARTICLE 8

WHISTLEBLOWING

8.1. Mandatory reporting of ghost workers, financial misappropriation, or violence.

8.2. Anti-retaliation protection for whistleblowers.

ARTICLE 9

DISCIPLINARY PROCEDURES

ViolationSanction
Ghost worker creationImmediate removal + EFCC referral
Data breachSuspension + NDPR penalty
Violence at eventExpulsion + ban
Vote buyingImmediate expulsion + INEC notification
ARTICLE 10

VOLUNTEER WELFARE

10.1. TSI shall not deploy volunteers to violent hotspots without security escort.

10.2. All volunteers covered by group personal accident insurance.

10.3. Equal opportunity for leadership; 50% women's representation target.

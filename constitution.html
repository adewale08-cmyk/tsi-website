The Constitution

Tinubu Solidarity Initiative (TSI) - Supreme Governing Document

📄 Download Full Constitution (PDF)

PREAMBLE

We, the members of TINUBU SOLIDARITY INITIATIVE, united by our shared belief in democracy, justice, and progress, do hereby establish this Constitution as the supreme guiding document of our organization.

Recognizing the importance of civic participation in governance in Nigeria, we commit ourselves to mobilizing citizens, promoting transparency, and fostering unity across ethnic, religious, and social divides.

We affirm our dedication to empowering youth and women, advancing community development, and supporting leaders who embody integrity and accountability.

📌 Article 1: Name & Identity

The Association shall be known as TINUBU SOLIDARITY INITIATIVE, operating as a voluntary, non-governmental, non-profit support Association.

Motto: "In Pursuit of the Renewed Hope Agenda: Good Governance Towards Nigeria Nationhood and Survival"

🎯 Article 2: Vision & Mission

Vision: To promote civic engagement, democratic participation, and good governance in Nigeria.

Mission: To mobilize citizens towards shared democratic values and national development goals.

👥 Article 3: Membership

Open to all Nigerian citizens aged 18+. Categories: Founding, Ordinary, Student, Honorary, Associate Members.

🏛️ Article 4: Leadership Structure

National Executive Committee (NEC) comprising President, Vice President, Secretary-General, Treasurer, and other officers. Three-year tenure, renewable once.

📅 Article 5: Meetings

General Assembly twice yearly. Executive Committee monthly. 14 days' notice for General Assembly. Quorum: 1/3 of registered members.

💰 Article 6: Finance

Funds from registration fees, donations, grants, levies, and fundraising. Annual audit required. Zero tolerance for financial mismanagement.

⚖️ Article 7: Discipline

Disciplinary Committee investigates misconduct. Fair hearing guaranteed. Sanctions include warning, suspension, expulsion, and legal referral.

📝 Article 8: Amendments

Two-thirds majority of General Assembly required to amend the Constitution. 30 days' notice required for proposed amendments.

🤝 Article 15: Conflict Resolution

Mediation → Arbitration → General Assembly decision (final). All internal remedies must be exhausted before external action.

✨ Guiding Principles

⚖️ Democracy & Rule of Law
🌍 Inclusivity & Equal Participation
🔍 Transparency & Accountability
🕊️ Peaceful Mobilization

Adopted: April 19, 2026 at Abuja, FCT, Nigeria

This Constitution shall remain binding on all members, officers, and organs of the Association until lawfully amended or dissolved.

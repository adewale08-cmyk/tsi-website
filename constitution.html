Tinubu Solidarity Initiative (TSI) - Supreme Governing Document

Adopted April 19, 2026 | Abuja, FCT, Nigeria

PREAMBLE

We, the members of TINUBU SOLIDARITY INITIATIVE, united by our shared belief in democracy, justice, and progress, do hereby establish this Constitution as the supreme guiding document of our organization.

Recognizing the importance of civic participation in governance in Nigeria, we commit ourselves to mobilizing citizens, promoting transparency, and fostering unity across ethnic, religious, and social divides.

We affirm our dedication to empowering youth and women, advancing community development, and supporting leaders who embody integrity and accountability.

In pursuit of these ideals, we pledge to uphold the rule of law, respect human rights, and work peacefully to strengthen democratic institutions.

This Constitution shall serve as the foundation of our collective efforts, ensuring that our actions remain guided by fairness, inclusivity, and the unwavering commitment to the progress of our nation.

ARTICLE 1

NAME, STATUS, HEADQUARTERS, AND IDENTITY

The Association shall be known as TINUBU SOLIDARITY INITIATIVE (hereinafter referred to as "THE ASSOCIATION").

Legal Status: The Association shall operate as a voluntary, non-governmental and non-profit/non-political support Association in Nigeria, in accordance with the Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.

Headquarters: The National Headquarters of the Association shall be situate in Nigeria. The Association may establish State, Local Government, Ward, and Community Chapters across the Country and in the diaspora as deemed necessary.

Motto: IN PURSUIT OF THE RENEWED HOPE AGENDA, GOOD GOVERNANCE TOWARDS NIGERIA NATIONHOOD AND SURVIVAL

Guiding Principles: Democracy and respect for the rule of law; Inclusivity and equal participation of all members regardless of gender, ethnicity, or religion; Transparency and accountability in leadership and governance; Peaceful mobilization and civic education.

ARTICLE 2

VISION, MISSION AND OBJECTIVES

Vision: To promote civic engagement, democratic participation, and good governance in Nigeria.

Mission: To mobilize citizens towards shared democratic values and national development goals. To promote political awareness and civic education. To support leadership ideals aligned with national unity, economic development, and democratic consolidation. To encourage lawful and peaceful participation in Nigeria's democratic processes.

Objectives: Political Mobilization, Civic Education, Advocacy and Policy Engagement, Youth and Women Empowerment, Community Development, Unity and Inclusivity, International Collaboration.

ARTICLE 3

MEMBERSHIP

Eligibility: Membership shall be open to all Nigerian citizens aged 18 years and above.

Categories of Membership: Founding Members, Ordinary Members, Student Members, Honorary Members, Associate Members.

Rights of Members: To vote and be voted for (except honorary and associate members); To participate in meetings, discussions, and decision-making processes; To access information and benefit from training, mentorship, and empowerment programs.

Duties of Members: To uphold and defend the constitution of the Association; To pay membership dues and levies as determined by the Executive Committee; To participate actively in mobilization, advocacy, and community development activities; To maintain discipline, respect leadership, and promote unity within the Association.

Membership Register: The Secretary-General shall maintain an up-to-date register of all members.

Termination of Membership: Membership may be terminated for gross misconduct, non-payment of dues for two consecutive years, or actions that bring the Association into disrepute. A member facing termination shall have the right to a fair hearing before the Disciplinary Committee.

ARTICLE 4

LEADERSHIP STRUCTURE

National Executive Committee (NEC): President/Coordinator, Vice President, Secretary-General, Assistant Secretary, Treasurer, Financial Secretary, Public Relations Officer, Organizing Secretary, Legal Adviser, Youth Leader, Women Leader, Director of Programs, National Spokesperson, Chief Security Officer.

Tenure of Office: All elected officers shall serve for a period of three (3) years, renewable once through democratic elections. No officer shall hold the same position for more than two consecutive terms.

Election of Officers: Officers shall be elected at the General Assembly through secret ballot. Candidates must be financial members in good standing for at least one year before the election.

Accountability: All officers must present annual reports of their activities. Officers may be removed by a two-thirds majority vote of the General Assembly for misconduct, incompetence, or abuse of office.

ARTICLE 5

MEETINGS

General Assembly: The supreme decision-making body of the Association, comprising all registered members. Shall be held at least twice a year. Quorum: one-third of registered members.

Executive Committee Meetings: Shall meet monthly.

State, Local Government, and Ward Meetings: Shall meet quarterly.

Emergency Meetings: May be convened at any time with at least 48 hours' notice.

Notice: 14 days for General Assembly, 7 days for Executive Committee, 48 hours for emergency meetings.

ARTICLE 6

FINANCE

Sources of Funds: Membership registration fees and annual dues; Voluntary donations from members and supporters; Fundraising activities such as rallies, lectures, and community events; Grants or support from lawful organizations; Any other legitimate source approved by the National Executive Committee (NEC).

Financial Management: All funds shall be deposited into a recognized bank account in the name of the Association. Withdrawals require at least two signatories, one of whom must be the President/Coordinator.

Audit: The accounts of the Association shall be audited annually by an independent auditor appointed by the NEC. Audit reports shall be circulated to all members and discussed at the General Assembly.

Accountability: Any officer found guilty of financial mismanagement or embezzlement shall be removed from office and may face legal action.

ARTICLE 7

DISCIPLINE

Code of Conduct: All members shall uphold the values of democracy, unity, and respect for human rights. Acts of violence, corruption, discrimination, hate speech, unlawful political activities or abuse of office are strictly prohibited.

Disciplinary Committee: The NEC shall establish a Disciplinary Committee to investigate allegations of misconduct. The Committee shall consist of at least five members, including the Legal Adviser, and shall operate independently. Fair hearing and due process guaranteed.

Sanctions: Verbal or written warning; Suspension from meetings and activities; Removal from office; Expulsion from the Association; Referral to law enforcement agencies.

Appeals: A member sanctioned by the NEC may appeal to the General Assembly within 30 days. The decision of the General Assembly shall be final.

ARTICLE 8

AMENDMENTS

The Constitution of the Association may be amended by the General Assembly. Any member in good standing may propose an amendment supported by at least ten members or one-third of the Executive Committee. The Secretary-General shall circulate proposed amendments to all members at least 30 days before the General Assembly. An amendment shall be adopted if approved by at least two-thirds majority of members present and voting.

ARTICLES 9-17

DISSOLUTION, INTERPRETATION, SUPREMACY & TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

Dissolution (Article 9): The Association may be dissolved only by a resolution passed at a General Assembly by two-thirds majority of registered members present and voting. Any remaining assets shall not be distributed among members but shall be donated to charitable organizations or civic education initiatives.

Interpretation (Article 10): The NEC shall have the authority to interpret the provisions of this Constitution. In case of conflict with national law, the Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria shall prevail.

Supremacy (Article 11): This Constitution shall be the supreme guiding document of the Association. All members, officers, and organs of the Association are bound by its provisions. Any action inconsistent with this Constitution shall be null and void.

Transitional Provisions (Article 12): Upon adoption of this Constitution, the founding members shall appoint interim officers to manage the affairs of the Association for a period not exceeding twelve months, after which elections must be conducted.

Sources of Income and Disbursement (Article 13): The Association shall obtain its funds from donations, fundraising, grants, contributions, and other sources that the Trustees see fit. All income and property shall be applied solely towards the promotion of the objectives of the Association.

Common Seal and Instruments (Article 14): The Association shall have a common seal which shall be kept in the custody of the secretary. All documents, contracts and instruments executed by and on behalf of the Association shall be signed and sealed with the common seal.

Conflict Resolution (Article 15): All conflicts shall be resolved through: Mediation → Arbitration → General Assembly decision (final and binding). Members must exhaust all internal remedies before seeking external intervention.

Oath of Office (Article 16): All elected and appointed officers shall take an Oath of Office before the General Assembly before assuming duties.

Miscellaneous (Article 17): Any matter not expressly provided for shall be determined by the General Assembly. The NEC may issue supplementary regulations. This Constitution shall take effect immediately upon adoption.

CLOSING DECLARATION

ADOPTION

This Constitution of the Tinubu Solidarity Initiative (TSI) was duly deliberated and agreed upon by the founding members. It shall take effect from the date of its adoption and shall remain binding on all members, officers, and organs of the Association until lawfully amended or dissolved in accordance with its provisions.

Adopted this 19th day of April, 2026, at Abuja, FCT, Nigeria

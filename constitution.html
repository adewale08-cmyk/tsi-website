Tinubu Solidarity Initiative (TSI) - Supreme Governing Document

Adopted April 19, 2026 | Abuja, FCT, Nigeria

PREAMBLE

We, the members of TINUBU SOLIDARITY INITIATIVE, united by our shared belief in democracy, justice, and progress, do hereby establish this Constitution as the supreme guiding document of our organization.

Recognizing the importance of civic participation in governance in Nigeria, we commit ourselves to mobilizing citizens, promoting transparency, and fostering unity across ethnic, religious, and social divides.

We affirm our dedication to empowering youth and women, advancing community development, and supporting leaders who embody integrity and accountability.

In pursuit of these ideals, we pledge to uphold the rule of law, respect human rights, and work peacefully to strengthen democratic institutions.

ARTICLE 1

NAME, STATUS, HEADQUARTERS, AND IDENTITY

1. NAME OF THE INITIATIVE: The Association shall be known as TINUBU SOLIDARITY INITIATIVE (hereinafter referred to as "THE ASSOCIATION").

2. LEGAL STATUS: The Association shall operate as a voluntary, non-governmental and non-profit/non-political support Association in Nigeria, in accordance with the Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.

3. HEADQUARTERS: The National Headquarters of the Association shall be situate in Nigeria. The Association may establish State, Local Government, Ward, and Community Chapters across the Country and in the diaspora as deemed necessary.

4. MOTTO: IN PURSUIT OF THE RENEWED HOPE AGENDA, GOOD GOVERNANCE TOWARDS NIGERIA NATIONHOOD AND SURVIVAL

5. GUIDING PRINCIPLES: Democracy and rule of law; Inclusivity; Transparency and accountability; Peaceful mobilization.

ARTICLE 2

VISION, MISSION AND OBJECTIVES

Vision: To promote civic engagement, democratic participation, and good governance in Nigeria.

Mission: To mobilize citizens towards shared democratic values and national development goals. To promote political awareness and civic education. To support leadership ideals aligned with national unity, economic development, and democratic consolidation.

Objectives: Political Mobilization, Civic Education, Advocacy and Policy Engagement, Youth and Women Empowerment, Community Development, Unity and Inclusivity, International Collaboration.

ARTICLE 3

MEMBERSHIP

Eligibility: Membership shall be open to all Nigerian citizens aged 18 years and above.

Categories of Membership: Founding Members, Ordinary Members, Student Members, Honorary Members, Associate Members.

Rights of Members: To vote and be voted for; To participate in meetings and decision-making; To access training, mentorship, and empowerment programs.

Duties of Members: To uphold and defend the constitution; To pay membership dues; To participate actively in mobilization activities; To maintain discipline and promote unity.

ARTICLE 4

LEADERSHIP STRUCTURE

National Executive Committee (NEC): President/Coordinator, Vice President, Secretary-General, Assistant Secretary, Treasurer, Financial Secretary, Public Relations Officer, Organizing Secretary, Legal Adviser, Youth Leader, Women Leader, Director of Programs, National Spokesperson, Chief Security Officer.

Tenure: All elected officers shall serve for a period of three (3) years, renewable once. No officer shall hold the same position for more than two consecutive terms.

Election of Officers: Officers shall be elected at the General Assembly through secret ballot.

Accountability: All officers must present annual reports and may be removed by two-thirds majority vote for misconduct.

ARTICLE 5

MEETINGS

General Assembly: Supreme decision-making body. Shall be held at least twice a year. Quorum: one-third of registered members.

Executive Committee: Shall meet monthly.

State/Local/Ward Chapters: Shall meet quarterly.

Notice: 14 days for General Assembly, 7 days for Executive Committee, 48 hours for emergency meetings.

ARTICLE 6

FINANCE

Sources of Funds: Membership registration fees, annual dues, voluntary donations, fundraising activities, grants.

Financial Management: All funds shall be deposited into a recognized bank account. Withdrawals require at least two signatories.

Audit: Annual independent audit required. Audit reports shall be circulated to all members.

ARTICLE 7

DISCIPLINE

Code of Conduct: All members shall uphold democracy, unity, and respect for human rights. Violence, corruption, discrimination, hate speech, and abuse of office are strictly prohibited.

Disciplinary Committee: The NEC shall establish a Disciplinary Committee to investigate allegations of misconduct. Fair hearing and due process guaranteed.

Sanctions: Warning, suspension, removal from office, expulsion, or referral to law enforcement.

ARTICLE 8

AMENDMENTS

This Constitution may be amended by the General Assembly. Any member may propose an amendment supported by at least ten members or one-third of the Executive Committee. 30 days' notice required. Adoption requires two-thirds majority of members present and voting.

ARTICLES 9-16

DISSOLUTION, INTERPRETATION & GENERAL PROVISIONS

Dissolution (Article 9): The Association may be dissolved only by two-thirds majority of registered members. Remaining assets shall be donated to charitable organizations.

Interpretation (Article 10): The NEC has authority to interpret the Constitution. In case of conflict with national law, the Constitution of Nigeria shall prevail.

Supremacy (Article 11): This Constitution is the supreme guiding document. All members and officers are bound by its provisions.

Transitional Provisions (Article 12): Interim leadership shall serve for no more than twelve months, after which elections must be conducted.

Conflict Resolution (Article 15): Mediation → Arbitration → General Assembly decision (final and binding).

Oath of Office (Article 16): All officers must take an Oath of Office before assuming duties.

CLOSING DECLARATION

ADOPTION

This Constitution of the Tinubu Solidarity Initiative (TSI) was duly deliberated and agreed upon by the founding members. It shall take effect from the date of its adoption and shall remain binding on all members, officers, and organs of the Association until lawfully amended or dissolved in accordance with its provisions.

Adopted: April 19, 2026 | Abuja, FCT, Nigeria

PREAMBLE

We, the members of TINUBU SOLIDARITY INITIATIVE, united by our shared belief in democracy, justice, and progress, do hereby establish this Constitution as the supreme guiding document of our organization.

Recognizing the importance of civic participation in governance in Nigeria, we commit ourselves to mobilizing citizens, promoting transparency, and fostering unity across ethnic, religious, and social divides.

We affirm our dedication to empowering youth and women, advancing community development, and supporting leaders who embody integrity and accountability.

In pursuit of these ideals, we pledge to uphold the rule of law, respect human rights, and work peacefully to strengthen democratic institutions.

ARTICLE 1

NAME, STATUS, HEADQUARTERS, AND IDENTITY

The Association shall be known as TINUBU SOLIDARITY INITIATIVE (hereinafter referred to as "THE ASSOCIATION").

Legal Status: The Association shall operate as a voluntary, non-governmental and non-profit/non-political support Association in Nigeria.

Headquarters: The National Headquarters shall be situate in Nigeria. The Association may establish State, Local Government, Ward, and Community Chapters across the Country.

Motto: IN PURSUIT OF THE RENEWED HOPE AGENDA, GOOD GOVERNANCE TOWARDS NIGERIA NATIONHOOD AND SURVIVAL

Guiding Principles: Democracy and rule of law, Inclusivity, Transparency and accountability, Peaceful mobilization.

ARTICLE 2

VISION, MISSION AND OBJECTIVES

Vision: To promote civic engagement, democratic participation, and good governance in Nigeria.

Mission: To mobilize citizens towards shared democratic values and national development goals. To promote political awareness and civic education.

Objectives: Political Mobilization, Civic Education, Advocacy, Youth & Women Empowerment, Community Development, Unity & Inclusivity, International Collaboration.

ARTICLE 3

MEMBERSHIP

Eligibility: Open to all Nigerian citizens aged 18 years and above.

Categories: Founding Members, Ordinary Members, Student Members, Honorary Members, Associate Members.

Rights: Vote and be voted for, participate in meetings, access training.

Duties: Uphold Constitution, pay dues, participate actively, maintain discipline.

ARTICLE 4

LEADERSHIP STRUCTURE

National Executive Committee (NEC): President/Coordinator, Vice President, Secretary-General, Assistant Secretary, Treasurer, Financial Secretary, PRO, Organizing Secretary, Legal Adviser, Youth Leader, Women Leader, Director of Programs, National Spokesperson, Chief Security Officer.

Tenure: Three (3) years, renewable once.

ARTICLE 5

MEETINGS

General Assembly: Twice yearly. Quorum: 1/3 of members.

Executive Committee: Monthly.

Notice: 14 days for General Assembly, 7 days for Executive Committee.

ARTICLE 6

FINANCE

Sources: Registration fees, donations, grants, levies, fundraising.

Audit: Annual independent audit required.

ARTICLE 7

DISCIPLINE

Disciplinary Committee investigates misconduct. Fair hearing guaranteed. Sanctions include warning, suspension, expulsion, legal referral.

ARTICLE 8

AMENDMENTS

Two-thirds majority of General Assembly required. 30 days' notice required.

ARTICLES 9-16

DISSOLUTION, INTERPRETATION, SUPREMACY & TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

Dissolution: Two-thirds majority required. Assets go to charity.

Conflict Resolution: Mediation → Arbitration → General Assembly.

Oath of Office: All officers must take oath before assuming duties.

Supremacy: This Constitution is the supreme governing document.

CLOSING DECLARATION

ADOPTION AND SIGNATORIES

We, the undersigned founding members of TINUBU SOLIDARITY INITIATIVE, do hereby adopt this Constitution as the supreme guiding document of the Association.
